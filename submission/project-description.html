The Clinical Context

Pre-Analytical Errors Lead to Sample Rejections and Delays

In clinical pathology, published literature highlights that pre-analytical steps (sample collection, labeling, tube selection, and handling) represent a major source of laboratory discrepancies and sample rejections before specimens reach automated analyzers. Typical workflow problems include collecting blood into incompatible additive tubes (e.g., EDTA instead of Citrate for coagulation assays), mismatched accession IDs between requisitions and vials, and underfilled tubes. In particular, underfilled Sodium Citrate tubes disrupt the necessary 9:1 blood-to-anticoagulant ratio, resulting in excess free citrate that can alter coagulation assay timings and necessitate specimen redraws.

The Proposed Solution

Automated Optical Scanning & Rule-Based Pre-Check Triage

LabSentry AI provides an interactive laboratory sample pre-check dashboard prototype. Running fully in the browser, the system combines client-side optical character recognition (OCR via Tesseract.js), image contrast pre-processing, deterministic clinical rule verification, and a human-in-the-loop review interface. The prototype cross-checks requisition IDs, sample IDs, tube additive types, and sample volume percentages in seconds, outputting transparent triage indicators—No Mismatch Found, Review Required, or Mismatch Detected—along with educational rationales for laboratory staff inspection.

Prototype Innovations

  • Privacy-First Edge Architecture: All image parsing, OCR, and rules run strictly inside the user's browser without sending demo label images to external cloud endpoints.
  • Explainable Rationale Engine: Avoids opaque outputs by pairing every validation result with an explicit, educational rationale regarding additive compatibility and fill ratios.
  • Interactive Synthetic Test Studio: Built-in 2D Canvas barcode label generator with 6 curated scenarios for repeatable demonstration without external image dependencies.

Limitations & Future Scope

Current Limitations: Evaluates simulated 2D label images and manual volume inputs; cannot measure physical clot presence or serum hemolysis, icterus, or lipemia (HIL indices).
