The Clinical Challenge

Pre-Analytical Errors Compromise 60–70% of Laboratory Results

In clinical pathology, up to 70% of diagnostic discrepancies occur in the pre-analytical phase—before specimens reach high-throughput automated analyzers. Common root causes include collecting blood in incompatible anticoagulant additives (e.g., EDTA instead of Citrate for coagulation assays), barcode misidentification between requisitions and vials, and tube underfilling. Specifically, underfilled Sodium Citrate tubes invalidate the strict 9:1 blood-to-anticoagulant ratio, resulting in excess free citrate that falsely prolongs PT/INR and aPTT results, risking severe clinical misdirection or unnecessary transfusions.

The Engineering Solution

Automated Multi-Layer Optical & Rule-Based Pre-Check Triage

LabSentry AI introduces an intelligent, browser-based pre-analytical specimen verification dashboard. Combining client-side optical character recognition (OCR), contrast-enhancing computer vision filtering, deterministic clinical rules (CLSI GP41), and a human-in-the-loop review interface, the platform intercepts labeling mix-ups, additive conflicts, and volume violations in under 2 seconds. Every specimen receives an unambiguous triage decision—No Mismatch Found, Review Required, or Mismatch Detected—accompanied by transparent clinical rationales and quality audit records.

  • Zero-Server Edge Privacy: All label image processing, OCR, and rules run client-side. Zero Protected Health Information (PHI) leaves the technician's workstation.
  • Explainable Clinical Reasoning: Eliminates opaque black-box decisions by providing clear CLSI-grounded biochemical justifications for every rejection.
  • Synthetic Label Studio: Built-in 2D Canvas label generator with 6 curated clinical presets for reproducible bench testing and technician training.
